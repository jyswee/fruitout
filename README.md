## Smash the fruit. Build the run.

Knock a bouncing ball into a wall of cartoon fruit and chase the **Juice & Combo**
score — every brick you break in flight stacks the multiplier until the ball comes
home. Then take it roguelite: push up a branching **Journey Map** across four worlds,
**draft** an upgrade after every battle, spend juice-gold in the shop, and dodge the
world bosses. One life bar for the whole run — clear World 4 or start over.

Pure fruit. Pure cascade. Pure push-your-luck.

## Why you'll stay

- 🧱 **Ball-and-brick, with teeth** — hard, tough, steel, bomb, and moving fruit-bricks across four world layout styles: Fill, Shapes, Maze, and Chaos.
- 🍓 **Juice & Combos** — points scale with the combo you build mid-flight; feed-the-score particles, screen-shake, and a satisfying bank when the ball returns.
- 🗺️ **Roguelite run** — a branching Journey Map, permadeath, and a HP-as-run-resource climb through Battle · Elite · Treasure · Shop · Boss nodes.
- 🃏 **Draft your build** — pick 1-of-3 upgrades after each battle (extra balls, wider paddle, fireball, steel-breaker, gold and combo boosters…) and stack a run-defining loadout.
- ⭐ **Classic mode** — the original 15-level, 4-world star campaign with power-ups and bosses, still there whenever you want it.
